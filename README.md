# Industrial Facility Lighting Design

### Diploma Thesis – Electrical & Computer Engineering

Complete lighting design study of an industrial production facility
using DIALux evo and AutoCAD.

## Project Overview

The project covers the lighting design of indoor and outdoor areas
of an industrial production facility.

The study includes:

- Production areas
- Warehouses
- Offices
- Laboratories
- Corridors
- Cold-storage areas
- Loading areas
- Parking areas
- Exterior roads
- Facility surroundings

## Software

- DIALux evo
- AutoCAD

## Standards

- EN 12464-1 – Lighting of indoor workplaces
- EN 12464-2 – Lighting of outdoor workplaces

## Evaluated Parameters

- Maintained illuminance
- Minimum illuminance
- Uniformity U0
- Unified Glare Rating (UGR)
- Luminaire selection
- Energy consumption
- Lighting layout optimization
